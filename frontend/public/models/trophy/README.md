# Season Wrapped trophy

Source: [Trophy by CreativeTrio](https://poly.pizza/m/fLy8KmmD1t) on Poly Pizza.
License: CC0 1.0 (public domain). Downloaded 2026-10-14.

Saved here as `trophy.glb`, split into three meshes by the colours of its
palette texture (`cup`, `base`, `plate`) with the texture dropped, so the story
paints the cup gold, silver, bronze or in the team colour. Stood 1 unit tall,
base on the ground, centred. Used by `src/utils/wrappedScenes.js`.
