What is
the NABS league?

A community-run online F1 2007 championship on Assetto Corsa. Four things to know before your first race.

01

A new era
every season

Each season we race a fresh grid of equally matched cars, right now F1 2007 on Assetto Corsa, with proper physics and no driver aids holding your hand.

02

Racing at
every level

Grids are split by pace so the racing stays close, whatever your speed. Reserves can step in for any team, and it’s a perfect way to get your first start.

03

A real
season

12 rounds on iconic circuits, run weekly. Your best 9 results count toward the title, so one bad night never ends your championship.

04

Built on
Discord

Everything happens in our Discord: sign-ups, banter, stewarding and results. This site is the scoreboard that updates itself after every race.
