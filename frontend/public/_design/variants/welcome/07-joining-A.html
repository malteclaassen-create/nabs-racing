Get on the grid

Joining is easy

No tryout, no pressure. If you can drive a clean race, there’s a seat for you.

Join the Discord →
  1. 1

    Join the Discord

    That’s the home base. Hop in, say hello in the intro channel and let us know you’d like to race.

  2. 2

    Get set up

    Install F1 2007 for Assetto Corsa and the car mod, then run a few laps. Everything is linked in the Discord and on Race Info.

  3. 3

    Sign up for a round

    Each race you mark yourself Accepted, Tentative or Declined, on the site or in Discord.

  4. 4

    Go racing

    Line up on the grid, race fair, and watch your name climb the standings after every round.
