Getting started

Do I need to be fast to join?

Not at all. The grid is split by pace, and the reserve system exists too, so newer drivers can find their feet against similar competition. Clean, consistent racing matters far more than raw speed.

Setup

What do I need to race?

A PC copy of the season’s game (Assetto Corsa right now), the current car mod, and a stable connection. A wheel helps but isn’t required. Mods, server details and setup help are in the Discord and on the Race Info page.

How long has NABS been around?

We’re 7 seasons in. Each season brings a new car era, a fresh calendar and a reshuffled grid. The community and the rivalries carry over.

Schedule

How often do you race?

Roughly one round a week across a 12-race season, plus the occasional special event like an endurance night. You sign up for each round, so you’re never locked in.

Schedule

What if I can’t make a race?

Just mark yourself Declined for that round, no penalty. Teams can call on a reserve to fill the seat, which is often how new drivers get their first start.

Points

How do the standings work?

You score points for your finishing position every round (P1 is 35 down to P18 is 1). Your best 9 results of 12 count toward the championship, calculated automatically and shown live on this site.
