How to get them on your phone ↓







  
Get them on your iPhone

  
It takes about two minutes and works with the free app Scriptable. You do this once.

  
    
    
  1. Install Scriptable. It is the free app that draws the widgets.
      Open it in the App Store, then open the app once.
    2. 
    
  2. Copy the installer. A short piece of text that fetches the NABS setup.
      Copied
      
    Show the text
    3. 
    
  3. Paste it into Scriptable and run it. In Scriptable tap + (top right), paste, and tap the ▶︎ button. It loads the setup from nabsracing.com and starts it.
    4. 
    
  4. Answer the questions. Your driver name, exactly as on the website (your name on nabsracing.com), then your series. If you race in Tier 2 you can choose to compare only with your own tier. The setup creates one script per widget, named "NABS · …".
    5. 
    
  5. Put a widget on your home screen. Press and hold an empty spot, tap +, search Scriptable, pick a size and tap Add Widget. Then press and hold the new widget, tap Edit Widget, and next to Script choose one of the "NABS · …" scripts. The sizes you see above are the ones to pick: small, medium or large.
    6. 
    
  6. Lock screen widgets (optional). Press and hold the lock screen, tap Customize, tap a widget slot, choose Scriptable and then a "NABS · …" script the same way.
    7. 
  

  
Good to know

  
    
    
  • You can stack several NABS widgets on top of each other and swipe through them.
    • 
    
  • iOS decides when a widget redraws, so numbers can be a few minutes old. The countdowns tick on their own.
    • 
    
  • A widget that looks old after an update: press and hold it, tap Edit Widget and choose the script again.
    • 
    
  • To update to a newer version, run the installer in Scriptable again.
    • 
    
  • Tapping a widget opens the matching page on nabsracing.com.
