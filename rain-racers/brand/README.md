## The look, as taken into the site

- **Black and white** like the logo: pure black pages, white type, white as
  the default league colour (the admin can switch it to a series colour, e.g.
  the red, blue or yellow of the RX8 Cup graphics; text on it turns black or
  white by itself).
- **The rain streaks**: the logo's rounded bars at 45 degrees, drifting
  slowly behind the home page's header.
- **Heavy, leaning capitals** (Exo 2, black italic) for headings, track
  names and round numbers, as in the race graphics.
- **The slanted colour panel** of the RX8 Cup graphics for the next race.
- **Round numbers in solid blocks** next to the track name, as on the DTM
  2002 calendar graphics.
