# Notes for Claude

## Changelog lines (the `## Changelog` section of a PR)

What goes in that section is shown on /changelog and posted to the league's
Discord as is. It must read like a league member wrote it, never like an AI:

- Plain, everyday words, the way you'd tell a driver in chat. Say what changed
  on the site, not how the code works.
- One short line per change. No marketing words: no "enhanced", "seamless",
  "streamlined", "improved experience", "elevate", "revamped", "robust".
- No emoji, no exclamation marks, no em dashes, no "We're excited",
  no "now you can easily…".
- No jokes, memes or puns. Keep it normal and to the point.
- The title is a few plain words ("A fresher look"), not a slogan.
- Leave the section empty if drivers don't need to know (admin-only, docs,
  internal fixes): it then stays off the page and out of Discord.

Good: `- Better: The menu marks the page you're on with a pink line under it instead of a box`
Bad:  `- Better: Tighter corners on the site. Shame about your lines through turn 1`
Bad:  `- Better: Enjoy a sleek, modern navigation experience with our refreshed menu! ✨`
